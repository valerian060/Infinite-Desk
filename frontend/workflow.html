The 10-Step Workflow of Infinite Desk

From sign-in to discovering hidden connections

🏠

Sign In & Enter Your Cosmos

You sign up or log in through the homepage. The backend authenticates your credentials against user data stored in MongoDB, granting you access to your personal cosmos.

🌠

Open Your Galaxy

After logging in, you land on your personal canvas, the Galaxy. All your existing ideas, called Stars ⭐, are fetched from MongoDB by the FastAPI backend and visualized as colored dots by D3.js.

➕

Create a New Star

You click 'Create Node' and add a title and content. This new Star is sent to the FastAPI backend, which assigns a unique ID and saves it to MongoDB. A new star instantly appears on your canvas.

📄

Attach Files to a Star

By double-clicking a Star, you can upload files like PDFs or images. These files are stored on the server, and their metadata is linked to the node in MongoDB.

🤖

Generate Its "Gravity" (Vector Embedding)

Whenever a Star is saved, the Cosmic Engine activates. It uses a Sentence-Transformer model to convert the text into a numerical vector called an embedding. This vector, representing the idea's mathematical meaning or "gravity," is stored in MongoDB's Vector Search collection.

✨

Form a Constellation (Automatic Clustering)

As your Galaxy grows, the HDBSCAN algorithm runs on all the vector embeddings. It analyzes the "gravity" (similarity) between Stars and automatically groups them into colored Constellations, revealing the core themes in your knowledge without any manual tagging.

✏️

Edit a Star's Content

You can double-click any Star to edit its title or content. The changes are sent to the backend, which updates the node in MongoDB and regenerates its vector embedding to reflect the new meaning.

🔍

Search and Discover

When you search, the system performs two actions: it highlights Stars with matching titles and also converts your query into a vector to find semantically similar ideas using cosine similarity. This allows you to discover related concepts you may have forgotten about.

🗑️

Delete a Star (Supernova)

Deleting a Star via its modal triggers a "supernova." The backend removes the node's data from MongoDB, its associated files, and its embedding, causing it to vanish from the canvas.

🔗

Find Hidden Connections (Conceptual Bridges)

The system automatically finds and draws dotted lines between Stars in different Constellations that are highly similar. These 'conceptual bridges' are found by running a cosine similarity query in MongoDB Vector Search for each node to find its closest relative in another topic, revealing unexpected connections.
