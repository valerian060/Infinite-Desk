3. AI-Powered Clustering & Mapping

Unclustered Ideas (Pending Analysis)

The core of our platform is a powerful Machine Learning (ML) pipeline. We use specialized models to convert every Node's text into a semantic embedding (a numerical representation of its meaning).

Then, advanced algorithms analyze the density of these embeddings, automatically finding and labeling coherent groups of similar ideas, forming clean, meaningful Clusters.

When you click Recluster, the Nodes that belong together gravitate toward each other, forming coherent, named Clusters based on this analysis.

Searching your knowledge graph activates Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). When you search, the system first retrieves the most semantically relevant Nodes (stars) from the graph.

The irrelevant nodes dim—like distant stars fading—and only the relevant context (including the active cluster and its related links) is kept bright. This filtered context is then passed to a powerful LLM to enrich the result, generating a grounded summary.

This process ensures AI responses are always grounded in your team's real data.
